# FRC Parts Finder - User Guide 🚀

## Welcome! 👋

You now have access to the most advanced FRC parts search tool with 10+ powerful features!

---

## 🎯 5-Minute Quickstart

### Step 1: Search for Parts
```
Type "NEO motor" in the search box → Press Enter
```

### Step 2: Use Filters
```
Click "Filters" → Select "Motors" category → Apply
```

### Step 3: Compare Options
```
Click "Compare" on 2-3 products → Click "Compare" in toolbar
```

### Step 4: Add to BOM
```
Click "Add to BOM" on your chosen part
```

### Step 5: Calculate Specs
```
Click "Calculators" → Use Speed Calculator
```

**Done! You're now a power user! 🎉**

## 🔥 10 Features You Need to Know

### 1. 🔍 **Advanced Filters**
**What:** Filter by category, price, stock status
**When:** Narrowing down 100+ results

### 2. ⚖️ **Compare Products**
**What:** Side-by-side comparison of up to 4 products
**When:** Choosing between similar motors/controllers
**Tip:** Compare specs, not just prices!

### 3. 🛒 **BOM Builder**
**What:** Build your robot's parts list with cost tracking
**When:** Planning purchases for build season
**Tip:** Export to CSV and share with team!

### 4. 🧮 **FRC Calculators**
**What:** Gear ratio, speed, and current draw calculators
**When:** Designing drivetrain or checking breaker sizes
**Tip:** Save calculator results in BOM notes

### 5. 💰 **Price Tracking**
**What:** See historical prices and get alerts for drops
**When:** Deciding when to buy
**Tip:** Look for the green "price drop" badge!

### 6. ⌨️ **Autocomplete**
**What:** Smart suggestions as you type
**When:** Don't know exact part names
**Tip:** Just type "neo" to see all NEO products

### 7. 🏪 **11 Vendors**
**What:** Search across REV, AndyMark, WCP, CTRE, VEXpro, Studica, and more
**When:** Finding best price and availability
**Tip:** Check "Vendor Breakdown" in BOM

### 8. 📐 **CAD Downloads**
**What:** Direct links to STEP, STL, and other CAD files
**When:** Designing robot in CAD software
**Tip:** Look for blue "CAD" button on cards

### 9. 📊 **Technical Specs**
**What:** Detailed motor specs (RPM, torque, current, etc.)
**When:** Making engineering decisions
**Tip:** Use in comparison view for best results

### 10. 👥 **Community Features**
**What:** Ratings, reviews, and robot examples
**When:** Learning from other teams' experience
**Status:** Coming soon!

## 💡 Pro Tips

### Tip #1: Minimize Shipping Costs
1. Add all parts to BOM
2. Check "Vendor Breakdown"
3. Order from fewest vendors possible

### Tip #2: Verify Drivetrain Design
1. Use Gear Ratio Calculator
2. Use Speed Calculator
3. Use Current Draw Estimator
4. Add recommended breaker to BOM

### Tip #3: Budget Planning
1. Create separate BOMs for different subsystems
2. Export each to CSV
3. Track costs per subsystem
4. Adjust based on team budget

## 🆘 Common Questions

### Q: Where is my data stored?
**A:** Everything is stored locally in your browser. No account needed!

### Q: Can I share my BOM with teammates?
**A:** Yes! Export to CSV and send via email or Google Drive.

### Q: Why are some prices different?
**A:** Different vendors have different prices. That's why comparison is useful!

### Q: Can I use this offline?
**A:** Yes, after initial load! (But search requires database load)
